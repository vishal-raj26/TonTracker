**Wallet Replay — understand the decisions behind your P&L.**

A closed trade shows the result, but it rarely explains it. You can get the direction right and still lose because you added size at the wrong moment, paid more funding than expected, or stayed in the position too long.

I’d add **Replay this trade** to every closed perpetual position.

Replay would place the actual trade on the chart and walk through it in order: entry, size and margin changes, funding, fees and exit. At each step, Wallet would show how the decision changed the position’s exposure and final result.

After reviewing the trade, the user could test one alternative, such as: **What if I had kept my original position size?** The alternative would appear in a separate Simulation view, while the real trade record remains unchanged.

Historical replay already exists on advanced trading platforms. Wallet can make it far more useful for everyday traders because the trade is loaded automatically with its real fills and costs. There is nothing to rebuild from screenshots or memory.

The first version could support one liquid perpetual market and only simulate decisions that Wallet’s historical data can reproduce accurately. Users could save one short lesson with the trade and see it before opening their next position.

Wallet already makes perpetual trading accessible. Replay would help users understand the decisions behind their results and improve how they manage the next trade.
