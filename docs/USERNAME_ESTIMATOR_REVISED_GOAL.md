## Goal text

Use verified completed sales, exact asset identity, economically relevant comparable evidence and the strongest estimator justified by the existing evaluations. Treat previous own sales as dated anchors, never permanent floors. Publish estimates with honest provenance, uncertainty and historical/current-market limitations; show unavailable when the evidence cannot support a numeric valuation. Never present an estimate as a live executable price or invent missing financial data.

Fix the remaining ingestion, caching, runtime, API, wallet and detail-screen integration issues. Preserve unrelated functionality. Verify representative historical behavior and all affected consumers, including ordinary names, premium names, absent or stale evidence, source failures and wallet changes. Run focused and full checks, document the results and save a durable checkpoint.

Completion requires a functioning, verified feature with truthful data and clearly documented limitations. A best-in-market claim, universal premium accuracy, new speculative model experiments and waiting for future multi-week research results are outside this implementation goal. Retain the rejected experiments and sealed research reservations for a separate research phase. Only username ingestion is authorized; no other workers, shared DNS schedules, deployment or subscription changes.

## Acceptance checklist

1. Complete a focused inventory of remaining implementation/integration defects from the current worktree; do not restart completed research.
2. Correct all defects found within the username feature and verify its affected callers.
3. Enforce exact asset identity, dated evidence, wallet isolation, unavailable states and honest valuation labels throughout API and UI paths.
4. Keep rejected research candidates out of production. Record premium accuracy limitations explicitly, without hiding historical failures or claiming they have been solved.
5. Run representative behavior checks and the focused/full suites. Record what those checks prove and what they do not prove.
6. Deliver the completed implementation, documented limitations and resume checkpoint. Record further premium-model research as deferred work rather than silently counting it as completed.
