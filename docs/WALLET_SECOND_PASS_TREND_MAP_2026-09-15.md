# Wallet contest second pass: 2025–2026 trend map

Research cutoff: 15 September 2026. Company adoption claims are labeled and are not treated as audited active users. The map emphasizes shipped behavior, not feature fashion.

## Executive pattern

The market is converging on five product shifts:

1. **Crypto disappears behind a recurring money job.** MiniPay sells global payments and local services; Phantom Cash sells one cash balance; Aave App sells savings.
2. **Stablecoins become payout and settlement infrastructure.** Visa, Deel, Airtm, Infinite and Stripe/Bridge increasingly let the payer use fiat while the recipient chooses stablecoin or local fiat.
3. **Wallets become programmable permission systems.** Spend limits, time windows, scoped signers, passkeys and recovery turn a wallet from a key container into a policy engine.
4. **Financial products become feed- and creator-shaped.** Base App and dub combine discovery, identity, social context and execution; the repeat loop is the feed and creator relationship, not the trade ticket.
5. **Local last-mile integration remains the moat.** QR rails, mobile money, bank aliases, cash agents and compliance determine whether instant stablecoin settlement creates actual user value.

## Early-source scan: what mattered

- **Product Hunt:** stablecoin wallets frequently launch with the same bundle—card, transfer and yield. Comments themselves ask what the added value is. This is useful saturation evidence, not representative market research.
- **YC directory:** W24–W26 cohorts repeatedly target remote-worker accounts, stablecoin payout infrastructure, local settlement and embedded beneficiary wallets. Accelerator presence proves founder activity, not product-market fit.
- **Reddit:** recurring first-person problems cluster around payment holds, FX leakage, missing local off-ramps, proof-of-income/tax records, wallet-address mistakes and revenge trading. These are anecdotes and may include promotion; repeated independent wording strengthens a hypothesis but does not estimate prevalence.
- **Official changelogs/docs:** the most credible “why now” signal is infrastructure readiness—virtual accounts, payout APIs, stablecoin settlement, smart-account permissions and passkey recovery are now shipped building blocks.

## What is hot but already crowded

Prediction markets, AI trading copilots, copy trading, stablecoin cards, generic DCA/rebalancing, merchant QR, cross-chain swaps and generic scam warnings are all active categories. Trend momentum supports timing, but it reduces originality. A contest entry must apply a trend to a sharper unresolved job.

## Whitespace types

- **A — solved elsewhere, not in Wallet:** worker payment links/accounts; programmable spending permissions; trade replay; hard trading limits.
- **B — poorly solved everywhere:** complete foreign-income journey from payer method through documentation to reliable local exit; recovery of exchange-returned funds.
- **C — enabled by new infrastructure:** user-readable permissions for apps/agents; fiat-funded stablecoin payout choice; passkey/recovery-based programmable accounts.
- **D — uniquely advantaged by Wallet:** sending a pay request inside a conversation, reusing verified Wallet identity and transaction history, and distributing to a large global messenger audience.
