# Product

## Register

product

## Users

TON wallet holders who want a fast, accurate view of tokens, Telegram gifts, stickers, historical floors, and recent market activity.

## Product Purpose

TonTrack turns a wallet into a trustworthy portfolio view. It prioritizes verified market data, immediate valuation, and useful collection and trait context without making users wait on live marketplace scans.

## Brand Personality

Confident, clean, and data-focused.

## Anti-references

Avoid noisy crypto dashboards, speculative values presented as verified prices, decorative finance UI, and desktop tables forced into mobile screens.

## Design Principles

- Accuracy before completeness.
- Show useful data immediately and load secondary context progressively.
- Keep dense market information readable on a phone without horizontal scrolling.
- Use consistent labels and source indicators across every screen.
- Make unavailable and estimated states explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, clear focus states, touch-friendly controls, and reduced-motion alternatives.
