# Rich App / Rich Dog / yRICH / Rich Treasury / Aurex 1
## Public-record due-diligence report — 5 September 2026

### Scope, method, and standard

This is an open-source investigation, not an allegation of criminal conduct and not investment advice. It uses public project pages, the live Mini App's delivered JavaScript, Telegram/X records, independent partner posts, domain/CT evidence, official exchange searches, public app analytics, and TON blockchain/API records. A claim is **VERIFIED** only when reproducible technical evidence or a source independent of Rich can establish it. “Not found” means it was not located in the sources searched; it does not prove non-existence.

The names are treated as one operating ecosystem only where the project itself links them: Rich Dog is the public Telegram game; the bot currently brands itself Rich App; the site describes a “$RICH Ecosystem”; yRICH is the yield-labelled in-app item; and Aurex 1 is the claimed model. This report does **not** attribute unrelated “Rich Dog” or “Aurex” tokens/projects to this project.

## 1. Executive verdict

**Verdict: materially misleading / extremely high-risk, with 88% confidence.**

The evidence establishes that Rich Dog/Rich App is a live Telegram Mini App, public channel, web site, and game/rewards operation. The second-pass technical investigation also identifies a wallet hard-coded by the app for crypto deposits and verifies substantial official-USDT activity, including repeated transfers explicitly labelled “Rich Ecosystem Withdrawal.” It does **not** establish a regulated operating company, identified leadership, an on-chain yRICH token, a segregated treasury, a public audited strategy, the economic source of payouts, an independently verified 124.1% return, or a demonstrably real Aurex 1 trading system.

The wallet evidence materially improves the answer to “are any payouts occurring?”: **yes, there is real on-chain payout activity.** It does not answer whether recipients are representative users, whether payments equal the advertised daily yield, or whether funds come from trading profit, game/advertising revenue, token purchases, reserves, or later inflows. Gross wallet turnover must not be mislabelled as assets under management, deposits, revenue, or profit.

The most consequential finding is not merely an absence of disclosure. Current marketing repeatedly presents yRICH as a daily-USDT, “guaranteed” 124%/124.1% product with no lock-up, whereas the current Terms say yRICH is, unless expressly stated otherwise in writing, a revocable, non-transferable in-game licence with no ownership, cash, token, profit, or future-payment right; they also reserve the right to change, suspend, revoke, recalibrate, and restrict withdrawals. That is a material conflict that a reasonable purchaser should resolve **in a signed, legally enforceable document** before paying.

What would materially improve this verdict: a named legal issuer and licence/registration details; independently audited financial statements; a complete, attributable wallet list; independently verified daily USDT payout ledger reconciled to treasury strategy P&L; public contract code and audit (if yRICH is on-chain); a named model owner/team; and a third-party performance report with gross/net returns, drawdowns, capital, venues, risk, and methodology.

## 7. yRICH, $RICH, contracts, and treasury forensics

### yRICH mechanics

The current site Terms classify yRICH among virtual currencies/points/items and, absent a separate written agreement, as a revocable in-app licence with no ownership, stored-value, debt, security, deposit, profit, or redemption right. The Mini App contains a second, more specific embedded yRICH agreement: it calls yRICH a non-transferable “digital membership entitlement”; says purchases are final, non-refundable, non-redeemable and have no NAV/buyback exit; says treasury assets remain the operator's property; and says yield is variable, can be zero, and is credited first as internal Stars (RSTAR), notionally valued at $0.01. These disclosures directly conflict with unqualified “guaranteed APY” advertising. [Site Terms](https://richdog.io/home/terms) · [Mini App](https://richdog.io/index.html)

The delivered client code handles `Player.YRichBalance` and `Player.YieldBalance` as numeric account fields. It hard-codes `YRichAPY: 1.241`, `YRichDailyYield: .0034`, `StarValue: .013`, `StarCashOutRate: .01`, and `YRichProductId: 10001`; successful purchases increment the internal balance. Withdrawals require a connected wallet, a minimum displayed yield balance of 770, and are described as taking up to 48 hours. The client calls authenticated backend endpoints such as `/market/createinvoice` and `/market/collectyield`; it does not expose an on-chain yRICH master contract, wallet contract, mint, transfer, or redemption function.

**Finding:** yRICH is best classified as an **operator-controlled, off-chain app entitlement/balance with a discretionary rewards programme**, not a demonstrated on-chain token. That conclusion is supported by both the legal text and implementation traces, not merely by failure to find a token in an explorer.

## 9. The 124.1% claim: construction, arithmetic, and plausibility

The project’s own Q&A characterises 124.1% as a **“current target yield”**, giving roughly $102 monthly and $1,241 yearly on $1,000. Other project posts use “124.1% APY,” “124% guaranteed APY,” “no catch,” and “paid daily.” The production client then hard-codes annual `1.241` and daily `.0034`; because `0.0034 × 365 = 1.241`, the implementation plainly derives the headline as a **simple annual rate**, even though marketing calls it APY. These meanings are not interchangeable. [Q&A/channel history](https://t.me/s/richdoggame?before=2192) · [Channel](https://t.me/s/richdoggame?after=2236) · [Mini App](https://richdog.io/index.html)

| Interpretation | Daily rate implied | 20-day return | Annual result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 124.1% simple APR, paid daily | 0.3400% | 6.80% simple | 124.1% simple; **~245.0% effective** if daily payouts are reinvested |
| 124.1% effective APY | 0.2214% | ~4.52% compounded | 124.1% effective |
| Project’s stated “124.1% base” becoming 145.2% with daily compounding | Not mathematically reconciled | n/a | 145.2% effective implies ~0.2455% daily, inconsistent with a 124.1% simple annual base |

The project’s $1,000/$102-month figure is approximately a 122.4% simple annualised payout; it is close to 124.1% only by rounding, but it does not resolve whether the public headline is APR or APY. No actual 20-day performance dataset, starting capital, net-of-fees return, drawdown, strategy allocation, or trade record was located, so a purported “20-day performance” cannot be independently evaluated.

A 124.1% sustained **net** annual return is far above ordinary cash/stablecoin lending and most unlevered blue-chip LP expectations. It could only be attempted through unusually high-risk/levered/illiquid/opportunity-constrained strategies, or a mixture of trading and non-strategy revenue; without exposures and P&L, the risk and source cannot be identified. It should not be treated as a stablecoin-yield equivalent to ordinary lending.

## 10. Sustainability model

At the advertised base rate, ignoring compounding, fees, defaults, taxes, reserves, and operating costs:

| User capital represented by yRICH | Needed annual payout at 124.1% | Average daily payout | Average monthly payout |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $1m | $1.241m | ~$3,400 | ~$103,417 |
| $10m | $12.41m | ~$34,000 | ~$1.034m |
| $50m | $62.05m | ~$170,000 | ~$5.171m |
| $100m | $124.1m | ~$340,000 | ~$10.342m |
| $500m | $620.5m | ~$1.70m | ~$51.708m |

These are obligations implied by the advertised rate, not estimates of actual deposits. The public holder counts do not disclose balances, and no treasury makes an estimate defensible. A programme can be economically sustainable only if it repeatedly earns at least these payouts plus costs/reserves from independently evidenced assets or revenue. No such evidence was located. It is therefore **unknown**, not established, whether new payments/inflows are required to fund distributions.
